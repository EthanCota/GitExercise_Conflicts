# Finance Track — Mac Studio (M5 Max / M5 Ultra) Lease & Financing vs. Cloud

Researched 2026-08-28 (Sonnet finance track). Hardware launched Aug 25, 2026; ships Sept 22, 2026 (per `research/gate0-verification.md`). All figures below are dated and cited; no application, purchase, preorder, or login flow was started.

## Top-line verdict

**Programs matching the user's framing ("$200/month lease, 6-month contract, vs. 12-month to own"): NOT FOUND for the new Mac Studio line.**

- No lease program — Apple's own or third-party — offers a **6-month term** for a Mac Studio of any configuration. The shortest Mac Studio lease term found anywhere (Apple Upgrade) is **24 months**; Apple's only 12-month product is **Apple Card Monthly Installments (ACMI)**, which is an interest-free purchase plan, not a lease, and it is not a 6-month product either.
- **$200/month lands almost exactly on Apple's own 36-month lease rate for the base M5 Ultra** ($110.10/mo) doubled toward mid-configs, and closely brackets a genuinely real number: **$398.91/month** is Apple's 36-month lease payment for the fully maxed M5 Ultra ($18,299 config) — i.e., real Apple lease pricing for this machine class runs from ~$49/mo (base M5 Max) to ~$400/mo (max M5 Ultra), and $200/mo sits mid-pack, not at any actual published price point.
- The one real-world product that *does* resemble "$200ish/month, 6-month commitment" is **Grover's consumer rental** of an old **M1 Ultra Mac Studio (2022)** at **€164.90/month (~$180 USD)** with 6/12/18-month terms — but that is a 4-year-old chip, not the new M5 line, and priced in EUR.
- The "$258/year" figure fits nothing found: not AppleCare+ ($64.99–$69.99/yr for Mac Studio), not any Apple Upgrade/ACMI payment, not a standard AI-subscription annual price (Claude Pro annual ≈$204/yr, Cursor Pro annual ≈$192/yr, Cursor Ultra ≈$2,400/yr). Flagged as unresolved rather than stalling on it (see Open Questions).
- Strong circumstantial finding: the user's "$200/month" almost certainly traces to a **different, unrelated frame** already in circulation in the press — "Can Apple's New Mac Ultra Replace Your $200/Month AI Coding Bill?" (Forbes/Yahoo, Aug 25, 2026) discusses $200/month as an **AI-coding-subscription** cost, not a lease payment. This is likely the source of contamination in the user's framing.

## 2. Cloud-equivalent spend at $200/month

### (a) Mac-in-the-cloud

| Provider | Instance / plan | Rate | What $200/mo actually buys | Minimum commitment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AWS EC2 Mac** (mac-m4max.metal — M4 Max, 128GB, comparable class to M5 Max Studio) | Dedicated host, on-demand | $6.25/hr | ~32 hours/month (about one 24-hr block + a few hours) | **24-hour minimum allocation/billing**, mandated by Apple's macOS licensing terms — you cannot release the host before 24 hrs elapse |
| **AWS EC2 Mac** (mac-m3ultra.metal — M3 Ultra, 256GB, closest class to M5 Ultra Studio) | Dedicated host, on-demand | $12.50/hr | ~16 hours/month — **does not even cover one 24-hour minimum block** ($300 for the smallest usable allocation) | Same 24-hr minimum |
| **AWS EC2 Mac** (mac2.metal, M2 mini-class, legacy/base tier) | Dedicated host, on-demand | $0.65/hr | ~307 hours/month (~13 days continuous) | 24-hr minimum |
| **MacStadium** | Mac Studio dedicated (Ultra-class, e.g. S2.M/S2.L plans) | $299–$599/mo depending on config | $200/mo doesn't clear their cheapest listed Mac Studio plan (~$299–369/mo for older Ultra tiers) | Monthly billing, annual commitment discounted 10–20% |
| **Scaleway "Apple Silicon as a Service"** | Mac mini M4 / M4 Pro (no Mac Studio or M5 tier found) | €0.22–0.49/hr | ~350–650 hours/month | Hourly, no long minimum, but caps out at Mac mini class, not Studio-class hardware, and M5 not yet offered |

Takeaway: **$200/month does not rent an M5-class Mac Studio equivalent in any Mac cloud provider surveyed.** It buys a day or two of the highest tier (AWS mac-m3ultra), a fraction of a month of mid-tier (AWS mac-m4max), or falls short of the cheapest published MacStadium Mac Studio plan entirely. Scaleway is the only provider where $200/mo buys substantial continuous hours, but only at Mac-mini scale, not Mac Studio scale, and Apple Silicon generation lags (M4, no M5).

## 3. Six-month total cost of ownership comparison

Basis: 6-month window (the term the user framed, even though no matching lease term exists).

| Path | 6-month cash outlay | What you have after 6 months | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cloud @ $200/mo (flat budget)** | $1,200 | Nothing owned; ~$1,200 of AWS Mac hours (~1 week continuous on M4 Max class) or ~500 hrs of H100 GPU rental or a Claude Max subscription | No asset, no resale, fully opex |
| **Apple Upgrade lease — M5 Ultra base ($5,499 list, $110.10/mo, 36-mo)** | $660.60 | Still owe 30 more payments (~$3,303) or must pay an early-exit fee up to the remaining balance to walk away at month 6 | Lease is a 36-month commitment; exiting at 6 months is not free |
| **Apple Upgrade lease — M5 Ultra 256GB/16TB ($18,299 list, $398.91/mo, 36-mo)** | $2,393.46 | Same early-exit-fee exposure; device not owned | |
| **Apple Card Monthly Installments — M5 Ultra base, 12-mo 0% APR** | $2,749.50 (6 of 12 payments of $458.25) | Own the device once all 12 payments complete; at month 6 you've paid half and are on-contract for the rest (0% APR, no early-payoff penalty structurally implied by ACMI's design, though not independently confirmed here) | Not a lease — a 0%-interest purchase plan |
| **Outright buy + resale after 6 months — M5 Ultra base ($5,499)** | $5,499 upfront | Apple Silicon Macs retain roughly 55–75% of value after 1 year (broad MacBook-based figures; Mac Studio-specific 1-year data not found) — a 6-month resale would likely sit in a comparable or slightly higher band, so a rough net cost estimate is **$5,499 − (~65–80% resale) ≈ $1,100–$1,900** net cost for 6 months' use, before wear/condition/model-year discount risk | Depreciation figures are laptop-derived proxies, not verified for Mac Studio at the 6-month mark — flagged as an estimate |
| **Outright buy + resale — M5 Ultra max ($18,299)** | $18,299 upfront | Same retention band applied: net cost roughly **$3,700–$6,400** for 6 months, plus far higher liquidity risk on a $18k desktop resale market | High-end configs are historically illiquid to resell at full proportional value — not verified for this specific config |

**Bottom line comparison:** the $200/mo cloud path is the cheapest 6-month cash outlay of all options ($1,200) but delivers no residual asset and, on Mac-class cloud hardware specifically, buys very little usable time. Every real Mac Studio financing/lease path costs more out-of-pocket over 6 months than the cloud budget, and none can be cleanly exited at 6 months without either owing the remaining lease balance (Apple Upgrade), being mid-installment-plan (ACMI), or eating first-year depreciation (outright buy). None matches "$200/mo × 6 months, then done."

## Open questions

1. **M5 Max max-config price (128GB/8TB)** — not published by Apple in any source reached; apple.com product pages were egress-blocked in this research environment. The $7,299 figure in the TCO table is an extrapolation from a base price + component upcharges carried over from prior-generation pricing, **not a confirmed Apple quote** — needs direct verification against Apple's live configurator.
2. **Whether the 128GB RAM tier still exists for the new M5 Max Studio** — Tom's Hardware (2026) reports Apple discontinued 128GB/256GB/512GB RAM tiers on *prior-generation* (M4 Max/M3 Ultra) Studios in March–May 2026 amid a memory ("RAMageddon") supply crisis. Gate 0 verification asserts 128GB is confirmed as the M5 Max ceiling at launch (Aug 25, 2026) — this should be treated as resolved by Gate 0's own primary sourcing, but the memory-crisis context means max-config *pricing and availability* could shift quickly; worth a spec-track re-check before finance figures are finalized.
3. **Apple Upgrade 24-month monthly rate for Mac Studio** — only 36-month figures were found published/quoted (base M5 Max $48.99/mo, base M5 Ultra $110.10/mo, max M5 Ultra $398.91/mo). No 24-month Mac Studio number was located.
4. **Apple Business Financing / CIT actual monthly quote for Mac Studio** — CIT's process is phone/application-based (1-800-854-3680) with no published rate card; this is a **login/application-required thread and was correctly not pursued** per task constraints. If real numbers are needed, this can only be closed by a human placing a (non-binding) financing inquiry call, not by web research.
5. **The "$258/year" figure** — could not be matched to any AppleCare, Apple Upgrade, ACMI, or common AI-subscription price point found. Recommend asking the user directly where this number originated before it's used in any further analysis.
6. **Mac Studio-specific resale retention data** — all retention percentages found (55–75% at 1 year) are MacBook-derived; no Mac Studio-specific resale-tracking source was found. The TCO estimate for buy-and-resell is therefore a proxy, not a verified figure.
7. **ACMI early-payoff behavior** — assumed no penalty (consistent with 0% APR installment plan design) but not independently confirmed against Apple's terms.
