## 8-Line Executive Summary

1. Legitimate minimum-spend methods exist for rent (Bilt, $0 fee), taxes (IRS: 1.75-1.85%), charitable giving, business expenses, group payments, and bulk household purchases—all zero-fee when using correct channels.

2. Tax payment fees (1.75-1.85%) require card earning >1.75% rewards to break even; federal threshold is $2,000 taxable income for 2026 1099-MISC reporting (up from $600).

3. Spend-required sign-up bonuses are NOT taxable (rebate treatment); no-spend bonuses ARE taxable; business card bonuses follow same rules.

4. Amex clawback risk is HIGH: 12-month closure triggers withheld/negative bonuses; clawbacks can close other Amex accounts. Chase has zero MS tolerance in 2026; 90% of MS methods are dead/detected.

5. Amex 2026 shutdowns (March wave) targeted resale/buying groups using sophisticated ML/AI detection; RAT team (Rewards Abuse) actively gates accounts with negative action.

6. Credit score damage from multiple new applications: hard inquiries ~5 pts each (12-month effect), new account penalty 15-25 pts total, recovery 3-6 months; 6 applications in 12 months = 25-45 pt loss.

7. Downgrading cards preserves account age/limit (zero impact) vs closing (utilization spike); utilization = 30% of score, but resets monthly—fastest-moving factor. AAoA recovery: 3-6 months.

8. Average APR 2026 is 19-25%; carrying a balance at 20% eliminates reward value entirely—paying $1,000/year interest requires $50,000 spend at 2% rewards to break even.
